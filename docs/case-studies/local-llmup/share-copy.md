## LinkedIn

Stop downloading weights your RAM can't fit.

local-llmup checks your GPU, VRAM, RAM and disk and gives every model in its offline catalog a yes / slow / no verdict with an estimated tok/s range, and the reason when it won't fit. Then it pulls, verifies and serves the one that does, across Ollama, llama.cpp, MLX and LM Studio.

The catalog: 66 models, evidence attached. Advice makes no network calls; servers bind to 127.0.0.1.

brew install shashankswe2020-ux/tap/local-llmup
