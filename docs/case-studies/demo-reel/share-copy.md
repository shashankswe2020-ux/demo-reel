## LinkedIn
Would your launch video pass 51 gates?
I built demo-reel, an agent skill that turns a repo into launch reels you can measure before you post.

The hook lab writes 10+ hooks and ships the top 3. Each one renders in 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9, and every render is scored on 51 machine-verified gates: motion in the first 0.5 s, -14 LUFS audio, captions, safe zones, and every on-screen number traced to a verbatim source.

After posting, it reads your analytics and names the winner.

npx skills add shashankswe2020-ux/demo-reel
